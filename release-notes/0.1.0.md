First release.

- **Add a posting**: paste a job posting (and optionally its URL). Deadline, salary, location, remote/hybrid/on-site and contact emails are read deterministically; one local-model call (Ollama, JSON mode) fills company, role, requirements, nice-to-haves and keywords, with the regex results winning on conflict and the model's dates re-parsed. Writes `Jobs/<Company> - <Role>.md` with the posting verbatim, a Fit list (plain bullets, never checkboxes, so nothing lands in your task queries) and a Timeline. Works without Ollama (regex pass only).
- **Change status**: wishlist → applied → phone screen → interview → offer → rejected → withdrawn; sets `applied_on` and appends a dated Timeline line.
- **Open the board**: rewrites `Jobs/Board.md` with what is due in 14 days, what has had no update in 21 days, and a table per status. Optional `.ics` export of upcoming deadlines.
- **Tailor resume to this posting**: resume bullets matched to requirements (quoted verbatim and checked against the resume), up to 5 keyword gaps, up to 3 suggested rewordings. The resume is never edited.
- **Draft interview prep**: eight likely questions with what each is checking; no answers are invented.
